Karo Health · Lamisil

Unlocking Full-Funnel Drivers and New Strategic Concepts

+

Challenge

Lamisil's positioning and communications skewed toward an older, medicalised audience. Broader brand strategy had identified an opportunity to reposition toward a younger, more active 18–35 audience, a group the category's existing tone actively alienated.

What I Did

  • I ran consumer and category research to understand what brought people into the category and the emotional barriers preventing treatment: younger consumers associated fungal infections with embarrassment, disrupted sport/social life, and a mismatch with their self-image as healthy and active
  • I identified the real entry points into the category for this audience: returning to sport, gym participation, summer travel, festivals. I fed that language directly into hooks and objection-handling
  • I replaced clinical framing with creative territories built around positive, active culture and sport
  • I mapped every territory against audience awareness stage (symptom-unaware through high-intent product comparison), briefed creative per stage, and used the same map to audit coverage and catch untested audience-and-stage combinations
  • I structured testing around specific, isolated hypotheses (e.g. active-lifestyle framing vs. clinical messaging for this audience) and read signal by convergence, counting an angle as proven only once it worked across several distinct executions

Results

14.25%ThruPlay VTR vs. 12% plan (+18.7%)
451,923ThruPlays delivered, 3% above plan
New angle"footcare as aspirational" identified, opening a new positioning route with professional endorsement and human reassurance as a lower-funnel conversion lever

Six creative territories mapped against five awareness stages: a structured matrix used to brief per stage, audit coverage, and catch untested audience and stage combinations. The standout result was a net-new premise: footcare as aspirational.

  • Synthesises consumer reviews, social conversations, search behaviour, competitor investment patterns and market trends to identify high-potential motivations, barriers, category entry points and messaging opportunities

  • Generates evidence-backed creative hypotheses, testing roadmaps, production briefs and UGC concepts with transparent confidence levels and source attribution
  • Distinguishes current and target audiences, preventing optimisation towards incumbent customers when growth depends on audience expansion
  • Separates performance-confirmed conclusions from inferential signals, improving governance around investment decisions and forecasting
